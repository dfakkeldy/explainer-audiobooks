# Pronunciation plan

- **Pictou** — `ˈpɪktoʊ` ("PICK-toe"). Dan's decision for the narrated edition: he rejected a
  "picktoau" rendering and required "PICK-toe" (Echo PR #468; recorded in
  `docs/nova-scotia-tax-sale-book/README.md` and the 54-figure publication receipt). Lexicon only,
  since it is said the same way everywhere.

Touquoy appears in the narrated edition, and Dan has said he does not know its canonical
pronunciation. If the textbook text keeps the name, it carries no markup until someone confirms it.
