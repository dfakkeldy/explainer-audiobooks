# Open questions before this edition becomes final

Collected from the per-chapter notes in `rewrite-notes/` and the figure designers' reports,
2026-10-23. The text currently follows the first edition and the evidence notes on each point, so
nothing here is less accurate than the published 2026 edition. The Nova Scotia Legislature and
CanLII sites refused automated access on 2026-10-23, so the statute checks are still to do.

## Statute wording to check (Municipal Government Act, consolidated 9 April 2026)

1. **s. 148, immediate payment.** First edition: "the whole purchase price or a smaller amount
   equal to the taxes, interest and expenses". Evidence note LAW-006 and `fig-payment-clock`: "the
   price, or at least the recovery amount". (Ch 10)
2. **ss. 150 and 155, certificate for older-arrears sales.** Does a non-redeemable (more than six
   years in arrears) sale also produce a certificate of sale before the deed? `fig-redemption-paths`
   draws a certificate for every sale, following LAW-007; Chapter 3 says it for redeemable files.
3. **s. 150, notices.** Does the treasurer send copies and a redemption notice to the owner and
   interest holders? Chapter 11 keeps the first edition's sentence; LAW-007 covers only the
   certificate and its registration.
4. **s. 151(c), insurable interest.** "Has" or "is deemed to have"? (Ch 11)
5. **s. 152(2)–(4), redemption amount.** (a) What the 10% interest runs on: the purchase sum or
   everything the purchaser paid. Chapter 11 says "that total"; the figure now says only "interest
   at 10% a year". (b) Which taxes: Chapter 3 says "the taxes the statute lists, including later
   taxes and interest". (c) The surplus and income adjustments reduce the amount (Ch 3, 11 and
   `fig-redemption-price`).
6. **ss. 152–153, new tax bills.** Are taxes the certificate holder pays during the six months
   repaid on redemption, and do the bills go to the holder? If not, Chapter 11's "pay the new tax
   bills" and `fig-six-months` should say "track".
7. **s. 156, deed effect.** "Evidence" or "conclusive evidence" that the sale rules were followed?
   (Ch 8)
8. **Marketable Titles Act s. 6(2)–(6).** Re-read the exceptions to the six-year window before
   print (Ch 12, `fig-after-the-deed`). LAW-018 asks for this too.

## Facts that need a source or a decision

9. **"Current municipal guidance warns bidders that clear title is not guaranteed"** (Ch 8) has no
   named, dated source in the research files. Name one or cut it.
10. **Pictou's "three days"** after notification versus the statute's three business days (Ch 10).
11. **Inverness's $200**: "registration amount" (evidence note) or "deed-registration amount"
    (first edition)? (Ch 10)
12. **Debit at Inverness** is accepted but is outside the statutory list of payment forms (Ch 10).
13. **HST sentence** in Chapter 9: the first edition's "whichever reading produces the lower
    ceiling" reads backwards; the rewrite says "the reading that happens to make the numbers work".
14. **The federal non-Canadian purchase ban** runs to 1 January 2027, ten weeks away. Re-check
    before publishing (Ch 9).
15. **Forrest Higgins** is named in Chapter 5's Touquoy example (a public court case, not a
    tax-sale owner). Keep the name or use "a landowner"?
16. **CBRM's clearance letter** wording: "building-code orders, by-law orders and fire-code
    violations" (source) versus "violations" throughout (first edition). Re-check the page (Ch 12).

## Editorial choices taken by default

- Chapters are about 85% of the first edition's length, not the 60–75% the brief aimed for: the
  repetition came out, and what remains is mostly separate facts. Each chapter's notes list the
  next cuts if a shorter book is wanted.
- Four chapter titles that were themselves qualifiers were renamed: 2 "Reading the Notice",
  6 "Access and Use", 7 "On, In and Under the Land", 8 "Title, Burdens and the People on the Land".
- The composite names (Quarry Lane, Cedar Street, Birch Point Road, Maple Ridge, Foundry Street,
  Harbour Park, Alder Crossing, Union Workshop, Meadow Line) come from the first edition. Harbour
  Park and Harbour Road are easy to confuse.
- The historical-results chart has no Inverness rows (the catalogue holds none) and shows CBRM's
  July 2025 sale rather than the March 2026 sample the text quotes.
