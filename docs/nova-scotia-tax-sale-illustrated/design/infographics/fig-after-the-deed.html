Drawn with the deed registered one year after the sale; the first year is enlarged. The six years run from registration, so the window moves with that date.
The registered deed

Vests fee-simple title, free of encumbrances

Easements and rights-of-way continue, as the statute specifies. The deed is also statutory evidence that the sale steps were followed.

Requested after the six months, or right after the sale when taxes were more than six years in arrears. The purchaser pays council's fee.

After the six years

The deed is generally binding and conclusive, with three carve-outs

  1. 1Land caught by the section's exclusion rule, where its assessment, interest and arrears conditions are met
  2. 2A current owner who took part in fraud or breach of trust
  3. 3A claim for damages for a wrongful tax sale stays open
If a court sets a sale aside, the municipal tax lien stays in place.
