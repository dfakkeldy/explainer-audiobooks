## Possible errors or unclear points in the original (flagged, not fixed)

1. **Immediate-payment wording.** The original says the buyer must pay "the whole purchase price or a smaller amount equal to the taxes, interest and expenses". LAW-006 and the `fig-payment-clock` draw note say "the price, or at least the recovery amount". "Equal to" and "at least" differ when someone pays more than the recovery amount but less than the price. The research folder has no statute text to settle it. I kept the original wording. Please check against MGA s. 148(1) and align the figure.
2. **Inverness $200 label.** The original says "deed-registration amount". OPS-004, `sources.md` and the figure plan say "registration amount". I followed the claim ID ("$200 registration amount"). If the December 2025 FAQ says it is for registering the deed, put "deed-registration" back in the text and the figure.
3. **Pictou "three days" against the statute's "three business days".** The original (following `sources.md` item 43) says Pictou required payment "within three days after notification". The statute gives an accepted tenderer three business days. I kept both as written. Please confirm whether Pictou's advertisement says calendar days or business days. If it really says three calendar days, that is a municipal term stricter than the Act, and it deserves a sentence of its own.
4. **Debit at Inverness.** Inverness lists debit, which is not in the statutory list. Presumably it is a method council authorized ("another legal-tender delivery method"), but neither the original nor the notes say so. The text keeps the two lists side by side without linking them.
5. **A bid exactly at the maximum.** "Each bid below the maximum … a bid above it" leaves a bid at exactly the maximum undefined. Chapter 9 defines the maximum as the highest amount, which implies a bid at the maximum is allowed. I kept "below" / "above" to avoid changing meaning. The author may want "at or below".
6. **HRM Charter sections.** I cited ss. 156–159 and 163–164, the sections named in LAW-005, OPS-006, LAW-015 and LAW-016. I did not use a continuous range, because nothing in the notes covers ss. 160–162.
7. **Cross-chapter consistency (outside this chapter).** The rewritten Chapter 1 says "Annapolis has moved properties that did not sell at auction into a tender." OPS-005 and the original Chapter 10 support only "March tax-sale results followed by June tender results". Whether the June tender held the same unsold properties isn't in the notes, so Chapter 1's sentence may be stronger than its evidence.
8. **The "Kings livestream" in `fig-auction-vs-tender`.** The figure's tender column cites a Kings County example (OPS-002) that the chapter text doesn't mention. That's harmless, but the figure is now the only place it appears.
