## Facts dropped or merged, with reasons

1. **Marketable Titles Act exceptions were stated twice** (original paras on registration and on the challenge). Merged into one list under "The six-year clock". The challenge section refers back ("the six-year limit described earlier, with its exceptions"). Reason: each limit once.
2. "A map user cannot determine fraud, breach of trust, damages or the application of an exception by selecting a parcel. Nor should a purchaser infer that the passage of time cures every problem or makes early title advice unnecessary." Merged into the careful panel ("The window is a reason to get title advice now… Counsel applies the timing rule and its exceptions to any claimed defect…"). Map-selection sentence dropped as a boundary restatement.
3. "Six years is not a title-review waiting strategy… cannot be diagnosed from the clock alone" was rewritten positively in the same panel. No content was lost.
4. "The centre is stronger than a map selection. It is still narrower than a claim that every ownership question has closed." Dropped as a boundary restatement. The title-file centre (deed for the correct PID plus the lawyer's interpretation) and what sits around it (easements/rights-of-way, court order, boundaries, registration requirements, unresolved qualifications) are kept, merged into the "three records" paragraph.
5. "A building inspection is not a title opinion. A service connection does not prove planning permission. A survey does not certify environmental condition. An insurer's offer does not make a proposed use lawful." Dropped. This is the banned "does not prove / does not establish" chain, and Chapters 5–7 already teach each pairing.
6. "Absence from a discovery layer remains only absence from that source." Dropped as a restatement of the Chapter 5/7 lesson. The surrounding instruction stays: go back to the official record, get an inspection or professional interpretation, and record what is still unknown.
7. "None of that proves the purchase was a mistake." Merged into "That is the earlier screen doing its job, leaving room for better evidence to change the next decision."
8. "Those allegations cannot be resolved by comparing the map to the notice and declaring the deed safe or void." The map comparison is dropped. Kept: the allegations need the complete record and legal advice, and a court may decide them.
9. "The recorded deed does not identify the legal status of the people inside." Merged into the possession panel, which says possession is a separate legal step that goes through counsel and lawful process.
10. "Those amounts can change… not a price copied from an old edition of this book." Kept as "those amounts change". The old-edition aside is dropped.
11. "This chapter does not need to inflate that effect or teach it again." Now "Chapter 8 covered what the deed does to title."
12. Closer: "It begins registration responsibilities, ordinary ownership decisions and a second due-diligence wave" and "The final task is not to memorize every branch." Dropped from the closer because the body covers each item. "Elena's separate file preserves the surplus route without turning it into a claim against the building itself" is shortened to "Elena's surplus file runs on its own track". Her "no claim against the land" is stated once, in her case paragraph.
13. The two right-of-way passes (original paras on the continuing right-of-way and on the "second pass") are merged into one paragraph: source instrument, route, purpose, maintenance, permitted use, the lawyer interpreting the instruments, and the surveyor relating the description and visible occupation to the ground.
14. **Addition:** "Had the property been redeemed, the balance would have reduced the redemption price, as Chapter 11 showed." This makes the text match `fig-surplus-route`'s draw note ("If redeemed: the balance reduces the redemption amount"). Support: LAW-010 ("adjusted for surplus") and original Chapter 11. It is a reminder, not a new claim.

## Flags: possibly wrong or unclear in the original

1. **Clearance-letter contents.** The original says the letter reports "building-code, by-law or fire-code violations". LAND-006 and sources.md #25 (CBRM page, refreshed 20 July 2026) say "building-code orders, by-law orders and fire-code violations". The rewrite uses the source wording. Re-check CBRM's page before print.
2. **"Strong binding and conclusive effect."** LAW-018 says the deed is "generally binding and conclusive" after six years. "Strong" is not in the evidence note and drops the "generally" qualifier. The rewrite uses "generally".
3. **Marketable Titles Act s. 6 not re-verified today.** The rewrite's description of the land-exclusion rule stays at the level of the original and LAW-018 ("conditions about the assessment, the interest and the arrears"). I tried to fetch the official consolidation on 2026-10-23 and the connection was refused. LAW-018's boundary note says to refresh the section number against the production edition. The consolidation used is dated 1 September 2015. Worth a lawyer's or editor's check of s. 6(2)–(6) before print, especially the exact trigger for the land-exclusion rule.
4. **Figure timing (`fig-after-the-deed`).** The draw note shows the surplus-claims lane "(up to 20 years)" on a timeline that starts at the deed request and registration. The twenty years run from the sale date, and applications open after the redemption period. The figure should anchor that lane at the sale, not at registration, or it will contradict the text and the `Try it` answer.
5. **Wording of who may apply for surplus.** The original says "a person claiming an interest in land sold for taxes". LAW-014 and `fig-surplus-route` say "a person with a prior interest". The rewrite keeps the original's wording. The two read as consistent, but a lawyer could confirm which one tracks MGA s. 147 more closely.
6. **Typo in the original:** "sixth- month anniversary" (a stray hyphen and space, and "sixth-month" for "six-month").
7. **Section headings:** the original's run-in headings ("The Document Changes, the Questions Move", "The Second Due-Diligence Wave", "The Money and the Challenge Take Different Roads") are replaced by sentence-case `##` headings, as the brief requires.
