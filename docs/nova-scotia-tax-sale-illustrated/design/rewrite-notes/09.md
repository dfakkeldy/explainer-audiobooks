## Possible errors or unclear points in the original (flagged, not silently fixed)

1. **HST sentence looks inverted.** The original says the file should get advice "rather than choose whichever reading produces the lower ceiling". A reading with less tax produces a *higher* ceiling (more room to bid), and that is the tempting one. The rewrite says "resist the reading that happens to make the numbers work", which avoids the direction. Dan should confirm what was meant.
2. **Cedar Street's occupancy.** The original's closing says "The occupants have not left", which implies the building is occupied. The same chapter treats occupancy as an open question, and the rewritten Chapter 11 says Cedar Street "appears vacant from the public road". The rewrite says "the occupancy question is still open", which fits both. Check this against the final Chapters 10 and 11.
3. **Federal prohibition date.** "Through January 1, 2027" is about ten weeks after today (2026-10-23), so the sentence may be stale by the time the book ships. Re-check before publication. Also, ELIG-001 says the prohibition is "extended to 2027-01-01" while the original says "through". The original's "through" is kept; the two differ on whether January 1 itself is included.
4. **The strip figure's count.** I wrote "close to 300 sales in nine municipalities". I read the working copy of `ns-marks-the-spot/SharedData/historicalTaxSales.json` read-only, without git, so I did not see the pinned commit 43e40b0b8. It has 274 sold rows with both an advertised amount and a winning bid, from Halifax, CBRM, Lunenburg District, Pictou, Cumberland, Clare, Queens, Victoria and Richmond, 2021–2026. Confirm the count against the pinned commit when the chart is drawn, and adjust "close to 300" if needed. Inverness is not in the catalogue, so the strip and the Inverness example are separate samples. The strip's denominators differ by municipality (for example, Halifax's "Opening Bid" against CBRM's minimum bid), so the chart should label each row's denominator. The "Comparisons like this need labels" paragraph points at this.
5. **Inverness surplus arithmetic.** The text says only that bids minus recovery amounts across the 31 rows gives "a different total". From the figure's totals that difference is $691,383.05 − $124,004.87 = $567,378.18, below the $608,693.21 reported surplus. That is consistent with four sold rows missing from the public sheet, but neither source reconciles the two. Nothing is wrong here; the number is left out of the text because the figure shows both totals.
6. **Chapter 10 cross-reference.** Original Chapter 10 says the conflict rule "does not answer the federal, tax, corporate-authority or representation questions from Chapter 9". Chapter 9 covers federal eligibility and tax, but not corporate authority or representation. This is for whoever rewrites Chapter 10.
7. **Payment detail.** LAW-006 says the purchaser pays the price, or at least the recovery amount, immediately. Chapter 9 keeps the original's broader "the required immediate payment", and Chapter 10 handles the detail. The MGA section numbers (s. 109(4) for the deed transfer tax exemption, s. 148 for payment) appear only in the source list.
