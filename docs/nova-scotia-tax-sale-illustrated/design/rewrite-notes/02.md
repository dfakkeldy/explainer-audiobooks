## Things in the original that look wrong or unclear (flagged, not fixed)

1. **Broken list.** The original's Harbour Road source table was one line of semicolon-separated items with stray hyphens (`- sale lien number — the event entry; - AAN — …`). It's rebuilt as a proper list with the same six rows.
2. **Retrieval date.** Harbour Road's file says "Packet retrieved July 20", but the real Inverness packet was retrieved on 19 July 2026 (Chapter 1 source list; DATA-002). Harbour Road is a composite, so its file date is fictional, but readers may take July 20 as the real packet date. I kept July 20. Consider July 19, or another date that can't be mistaken for the real one.
3. **"Redeemed … before or around the event."** In this book "redeem" is a post-sale term (Chapter 3). Before the sale the arrears are paid. The wording comes from OPS-003 (CBRM's result legend), so it's kept, but it reads oddly next to this chapter's own definition of redemption.
4. **`fig-recovery-amounts` caption.** The caption gives the range as "$879.69 to $57,875.01". The top value is lien 12's summary amount, and lien 12 is one of the two disputed rows: its detail sheet says $57,578.01 (`research/inverness-county-dossier.md`). The draw notes say the plot uses the summary amounts, but the caption doesn't say so, and this chapter teaches that the summary and detail amounts differ. Suggest a caption change: "summary recovery amounts ranged from…". The median and total probably also come from the summary.
5. **Exact amounts.** The dossier has the lien 9 and 12 figures ($9,348.38 vs $9,384.38; $57,875.01 vs $57,578.01). The original chapter text says only "two entries", and so does the rewrite. The `fig-inverness-reconcile` caption names liens 9 and 12.
6. **Source for AAN.** For AAN and PID the evidence notes cite "Nova Scotia Deed Transfer Affidavit information" (LAND-001). It's kept in the source list, but it's an odd primary source for what an AAN is. PVSC's own pages would read better if they were verified.
7. **PVSC abbreviation in figures.** The `fig-packet-anatomy` and `fig-which-key` draw notes use "PVSC", but the chapter text uses only the full name, Property Valuation Services Corporation. Either the figure spells it out or the glossary footer needs to carry it.
8. **Statute sections.** The source list cites MGA ss. 141, 143 and 148 for the recovery amount, taken from DATA-001. The chapter never quotes those sections, so they support the "taxes, interest and expenses" meaning only through the evidence notes.
