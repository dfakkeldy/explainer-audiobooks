## Unclear or possibly wrong in the original (not fixed in the facts)

1. **"The municipality does not provide access."** On its own it can be read as "the municipality gives no legal road access to the parcel". LAND-007 shows the CBRM statement is about entry: the municipality does not own listed properties and cannot authorize a bidder to enter. The rewrite says "does not provide access to listed properties" and pairs it with the no-entry rule. Worth checking against the current CBRM page.
2. **"A tax deed may later change the purchaser's registered interest according to the governing process."** Vague. LAW-012 states it cleanly: the deed vests fee-simple title free of encumbrances, while appurtenant and burdening easements and rights-of-way continue as the statute specifies (MGA ss. 155–156). The wording is kept; Dan may prefer the LAW-012 sentence.
3. **"The deed branch from Chapter 3 therefore cannot cure this problem by anticipation."** Opaque; merged as above.
4. **"The authorized record search"**: the original never says who authorizes it (a lawyer's title search? a Property Online subscription?). Kept as written.
5. **Is Maple Ridge in Inverness County?** The original moves from "In Inverness County…" to "They do not pre-approve Maple Ridge", which implies it, but never says so. The rewrite keeps the Inverness paragraph general.
6. **`fig-access-plan` caption vs its draw notes.** The caption's second clause, "a parcel beside a road may have no registered right to use it", has no matching parcel in the drawing. Parcel A fronts the road with legal frontage; B, D and E are set back. Either add a parcel that touches the road without frontage (the Maple Ridge corner case) or reword the caption. I left `design/figures.yaml` alone.
7. The original's Case A figure alt text mentions "steep contours" and "three fictional record identifiers"; neither is in the chapter text, so neither is carried into the prose.
