The two sentences with a negative, and why each stays:

1. Careful panel 1: "Paying the purchase money does not bring vacant possession, and the municipality does not remove residents or provide access." A buyer who thinks payment brings an empty building, or that the municipality will clear it, is the mistake that costs rights and invites self-help.
2. "The Act also requires her to insure buildings that are insurable, but it cannot make an insurer say yes." The duty-versus-availability point. A reader who confuses them either assumes coverage exists or abandons the duty.

The other real prohibitions are stated once each without a counted negative: tree cutting and injuring the premises ("The Act also forbids…"), unapproved repairs (careful panel 2: "Treat any repair made without it as your own cost"), the moment rights end ("Maya's interest survives a message… and it survives a part payment"), and what Maya may do after her rights end ("Maya has no authority to hold the property, demand extra money or enter the premises").

## Possible errors or unclear points in the original (not fixed silently)

1. **"serves the statutory copies and redemption notice."** Kept as "sends out the copies and redemption notice the statute requires". LAW-007's supported wording (MGA s. 150; HRMC s. 165) covers only giving the certificate to the purchaser and registering a copy. Check s. 150 for a duty to send copies and a redemption notice to the owner and interest holders, and who must receive them.
2. **Who bears the new taxes.** The original tells Maya to confirm "what must be paid when due", and the figure plan has a "pay new tax bills" band, so the rewrite lists "pay the new tax bills when due" as one of the five jobs. But the original's purchaser-repayment list (purchase sum, interest, fire-insurance premiums, approved repairs, less income) has no taxes in it, while the redemption amount includes "taxes levied after the sale and related interest". The original also lists "taxes" among costs the purchaser carries regardless. It is unclear whether a certificate holder who pays post-sale taxes is repaid them on redemption, and whether the bills go to the holder or stay on the former owner's account. Check MGA ss. 152(2) and 153. If the holder is not billed, "pay the new tax bills" in the list and the figure should become "track the new tax bills".
3. **"Deemed" insurable interest.** The original says the Act "says Maya is deemed to have an insurable interest". INS-001 says the holder "has an insurable interest" (s. 151(c)). The rewrite keeps "deems". Check the statute's verb.
4. **Fire insurance versus the policy Maya buys.** The redemption formula and purchaser's statement cover "fire-insurance premiums"; the duty is to insure buildings. A vacant-building or non-standard policy may cover more than fire. Whether its whole premium qualifies is not addressed. The rail now says "fire-insurance premium receipt" to stay within the statute's category.
5. **"Tax-sale surplus account" first appears here.** Chapter 12 (rewrite) bolds and defines it, and says "as Chapter 11 showed" about the surplus reducing the redemption amount. Book order puts the first use in Chapter 11. Either move the bold definition to Chapter 11 or leave Chapter 11's use as a plain mention; I left it plain to avoid editing Chapter 12's definition.
6. **Surplus deduction.** The original says the surplus balance is deducted from the redemption amount (LAW-010: "adjusted for surplus"). Chapter 3's notes ask for the direction to be confirmed against s. 152(3)–(4); the same check covers this chapter.
7. **Ten percent on "the total paid by the purchaser".** Kept as "on that total". LAW-010 says "ten percent annual interest from sale to redemption" without saying on what base. Check s. 152(2) for whether interest runs on the purchase sum only or on everything the purchaser paid.
