# Sources and credits

## Where the facts come from

Each chapter ends with its own list of official sources. The main ones are:

- the Municipal Government Act, consolidated to 9 April 2026, and the Halifax Regional Municipality Charter, consolidated to 1 May 2026, both from the Nova Scotia Legislature: nslegislature.ca;
- the Marketable Titles Act, as consolidated on 1 September 2015;
- municipal tax-sale pages, notices, packets, minutes and result sheets from Inverness, Cape Breton Regional Municipality, Halifax, Richmond, Pictou, Annapolis, Kings, Chester and Victoria, each dated in the text;
- the Province's land-information services: Property Online, the Nova Scotia property boundary and aerial imagery services, and Property Valuation Services Corporation's public assessment search.

The full research record, with every claim and its source, is public: github.com/dfakkeldy/explainer-audiobooks, in the folder docs/nova-scotia-tax-sale-book.

## Charts and diagrams

The charts of past sale results use the municipalities' own published results, compiled in NS Marks The Spot's results catalogue (source revision 43e40b0b8, October 2026). Rows with an unknown, withdrawn or unsold outcome are left out of the ratio charts. The diagrams of the legal process follow the statute sections named in each chapter's sources.
