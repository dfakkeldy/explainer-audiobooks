# The Twelve-Week Runway Replacement Design

**Date:** 2026-07-14  
**Status:** Approved for implementation planning  
**Privacy:** Private learning package; no manuscript, research notes, or generated media enters the public repository

## Context

The twelve-week Claude Certified Architect series introduction was produced as
*Before the Blueprint*. An earlier, separate introduction for the four-week
training series already uses that title and already covers the credential,
eligibility, exam shape, domains, prerequisites, and general study method. The
collision is therefore editorial as well as bibliographic.

The correction must create a genuinely distinct introduction for the
twelve-week series. Renaming the current package without changing its learning
job would leave substantial duplication and would not solve the underlying
problem.

## Considered Approaches

### 1. Full differentiation — selected

Replace most of the current introduction with a 45–55 minute operating manual
for the twelve-week program. Keep only the credential context needed to orient
the listener, then focus on cadence, practice, evidence, recovery, and the path
into Week 1.

This approach best protects the value of the older book and gives the new
series a clear entry point.

### 2. Surgical rewrite

Keep the existing eight-chapter structure, change the title, and rewrite only
the most overlapping sections. This is faster but leaves two introductions
performing nearly the same job.

### 3. Trailer instead of an introduction

Retire the long introduction and publish a short series trailer or listening
guide before Week 1. This removes duplication most aggressively but loses the
substantive learning-system orientation Dan requested.

## Selected Identity

- **Title:** *The Twelve-Week Runway*
- **Subtitle:** *How to Use the Claude Architect's Workshop*
- **Series:** *The Claude Architect's Workshop*
- **Position:** Volume 0 of a private thirteen-volume sequence
- **Listener:** An adjacent practitioner who is experienced with coding agents
  and shipping software but has gaps in the raw Claude API, Agent SDK, MCP, and
  current certification mechanics
- **Target runtime:** 45–55 minutes
- **Narrator:** Native Echo/Kokoro `am_michael`

## Editorial Promise

By the end of the book, the listener can run the twelve-week course as a
sustainable practice system. They will know what to do before Week 1, how each
week converts listening into hands-on evidence, how to recover from a missed
week without cramming, and how to judge readiness without mistaking recognition
for competence.

The book is not a second general credential guide. It gives a concise account
of the credential only where that context explains the design of the twelve-week
program. Detailed credential meaning, eligibility, exam shape, domain tour, and
generic reasons to certify remain the learning job of the earlier four-week
series introduction.

## Proposed Learning Arc

1. **Why Twelve Weeks** — distinguish calendar time from learning time and
   explain why the slower runway exists.
2. **Set the Baseline** — create a practical gap map from current experience,
   without turning the opening week into a mock exam.
3. **The Weekly Operating Loop** — listen, retrieve, build, explain, and record
   evidence; define a minimum viable week and a deeper week.
4. **The Twelve-Volume Map** — show how the weekly books progress from the
   execution loop through tools, MCP, Claude Code, prompting, context,
   reliability, evaluation, security, and integrated architecture.
5. **Build an Evidence Trail** — keep small artifacts, decision records,
   failure notes, and teach-back summaries that make progress observable.
6. **When Real Life Breaks the Schedule** — use recovery rules, consolidation
   weeks, and stop conditions instead of doubling the next workload.
7. **Readiness and the Week 1 Handoff** — define architecture-level readiness,
   set the first lab, and hand the listener directly to *The Loop Beneath the
   Conversation*.

Chapter titles may become more literary during drafting, but their learning jobs
and order remain fixed.

## Source and Freshness Model

- Use the existing series source dossier and the SGridworks twelve-week and
  four-week training repositories as curriculum maps.
- Follow their linked learning materials to primary Anthropic documentation,
  announcements, Academy material, and credential information.
- Treat community repositories as useful maps, not as authoritative exam
  specifications.
- Refresh all drift-prone certification and product claims before canonical
  drafting.
- Do not reproduce leaked questions, recalled exam items, or proprietary
  assessment content. All practice scenarios are original.

## Cover Direction and Human Gate

The new title receives exactly three coordinated portrait/square cover pairs.
They must visually signal preparation, measured progress, and a twelve-stage
journey without reusing the old blueprint metaphor. At least one direction is
bright or high-key. Generated source art remains text-free; typography is
applied through the governed paired-cover renderer.

Production pauses after the three complete pairs are shown. Dan explicitly
selects one pair or requests a mix before the EPUB and M4B are built.

## Manuscript and Audio Gates

- One frontier author owns all canonical Markdown and substantive revisions.
- Maintain a coverage ledger and continuity record.
- Run citation-first editorial review, prose QC, bounded humanization, and the
  final hash-bound de-Claudification gate before packaging.
- Build a fresh EPUB and combined Markdown from the accepted manuscript.
- Render a fresh native Echo M4B using the governed wrapper and the approved
  Echo revision. Never edit the audited M4B after narration.
- Verify the paired cover receipt, Echo success receipt, pronunciation audit,
  alignment sidecar, duration, hashes, and delivery manifest before copying.
- Keep human listening status `pending` until the pronunciation reel or matching
  final passages have actually been heard.

## Series Canon Changes

Update the private longform workspace so Volume 0 is *The Twelve-Week Runway*
and all future handoffs use the new slug and editorial promise. Record
*Before the Blueprint* as the distinct earlier four-week-series introduction,
not as an alternate title for the twelve-week book.

## Success Criteria

The replacement is complete when:

1. Its chapter-level content is materially distinct from the earlier book.
2. The twelve-week series brief, outline, conversation log, visual manifest,
   and handoff packet consistently name the new introduction.
3. Dan has explicitly selected one of three new cover pairs.
4. The canonical manuscript passes source, prose, narration, and style gates.
5. A fresh EPUB, Markdown, native Echo M4B, alignment sidecar, pronunciation
   artifacts, receipts, and manifest pass governed verification.
6. The verified package exists in the new iCloud Books folder.
7. Both old packages remain recoverable, and the incorrect twelve-week edition
   is clearly marked as superseded rather than silently destroyed.
