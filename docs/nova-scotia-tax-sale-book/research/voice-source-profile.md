# Voice source profile

Source: project-authored learning brief; no private voice source was supplied.

- Opening move: begin at an auction table moments before a parcel is called, then move backward through the months of municipal procedure that made the moment possible.
- Evidence-to-example movement: state the rule in ordinary language, locate it in the statute or official process, then test it against a fictional composite property.
- Plain-language mechanism: explain every document by the job it performs and every clock by what changes when it expires.
- Direct address: use “you” for observation and decision discipline, never to issue personalized legal or investment instructions.
- Humor boundary: light observational humour about paperwork and auction adrenaline is acceptable; no humour at the expense of owners, occupants, heirs, or people in arrears.
- Uncertainty: distinguish known, discoverable, professionally verifiable, and irreducibly uncertain facts. Never turn a missing record into a reassuring inference.
- Rhythm: warm, calm, varied sentences; frequent concrete resets to one parcel, one document, one clock, or one decision.
- Practical landing: each chapter ends with a changed ability, not a checklist recital.
- Visual-to-audio adaptation: tables, parcel maps, title chains and cost worksheets become short spoken walks; detailed forms and live links belong in a non-narrated reference appendix.
- Constraint: craft features, not pastiche. The accepted project-authored first section will become the voice exemplar.
