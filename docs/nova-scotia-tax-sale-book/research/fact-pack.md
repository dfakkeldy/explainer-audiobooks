# Chapter fact pack

This is a chapter-facing selection from `evidence-notes.md`; the stable claim IDs remain the authority.

## 1. The auction is the last scene

- `LAW-001`, `LAW-002`, `LAW-003`, `LAW-004`: first lien, eligibility, notices, title search and advertisement.
- `OPS-004`, `OPS-005`, `DATA-002`, `DATA-005`: Inverness County's current packet and Port Hood auction are the principal municipal worked examples. The packet already does substantial orientation work; municipality-specific procedures remain live and variable.
- Human frame: the assessed owner, lienholders, municipal treasurer and possible occupants all enter the story before the bidder does.

## 2. Read the notice without hallucinating a property

- `LAND-001`, `LAND-002`, `LAND-003`, `LAND-004`: AAN, PID, Property Online, assessment and map limitations.
- `OPS-003`, `DATA-002`: the list remains live and can shrink, and a public packet may contain internal differences that must be reconciled before use.

## 3. Two clocks after the hammer

- `LAW-007` through `LAW-012`: certificate, interim rights, redemption formula, deed timing and deed effect.
- `OCC-001`: neither clock promises vacant possession.

## 4. Build the parcel evidence file

- `DATA-005`, `LAND-001` through `LAND-007`, `GIS-001`, `GIS-002`: begin with the municipality's existing fact table, aerial/parcel map and legal description, then add identity/title hierarchy, lawful public map screening, legal access, planning, municipal clearance and the no-trespass rule.

## 5. Can the land do the job?

- `LAND-005`, `LAND-006`, `LAND-008`: legal access, frontage, Plan Inverness planning controls, servicing and permit status.
- Development goal must be written before research; otherwise “buildable” has no defined meaning.

## 6. What may be on, in or under it?

- `ENV-001` through `ENV-005`: contamination, wells, sewage records, coast and abandoned mines.
- No negative-result search proves physical condition.

## 7. Title, easements and people in possession

- `LAW-003`, `LAW-008`, `LAW-012`, `LAW-013`, `OCC-001`, `MOB-001`.
- Keep three questions separate: what interest the deed conveys; what burdens continue; who is physically or lawfully in possession.

## 8. Price the uncertainty, not the excitement

- `DATA-001` through `DATA-004`, `TAX-001`, `TAX-002`, `TAX-003`, `ELIG-001`.
- Educational all-in model: bid + possible HST + possible provincial non-resident deed transfer tax + legal/registry/survey costs + carrying costs + safety/security + repairs/remediation + possession process + development work + uncertainty reserve. A tax-sale deed is exempt from municipal deed transfer tax under MGA s. 109(4), while the Affidavit of Value remains part of registration.
- This model teaches categories, not a personalized bid.

## 9. Auction and tender day

- `LAW-005`, `LAW-006`, `OPS-001` through `OPS-005`, `DATA-001`, `DATA-002`.
- A written maximum is decided before bidding. Payment readiness is part of eligibility, not an after-sale task.

## 10. The certificate-holder months

- `LAW-007` through `LAW-011`, `OCC-001`.
- Protect, insure if possible, pay new tax bills, document qualifying expenses, avoid diminishment and do not confuse protection with redevelopment.

## 11. Deed, possession and the second due-diligence wave

- `LAW-012`, `LAND-005`, `LAND-006`, `ENV-001`, `OCC-001`, `TAX-001`.
- The deed resolves the statutory sale stage; it starts rather than ends many owner responsibilities.

## 12. A repeatable decision record

- All claim families.
- Final ability: classify every item as verified, professionally verifiable, unresolved but priceable, or disqualifying; record a go/no-go decision without needing auction adrenaline.
