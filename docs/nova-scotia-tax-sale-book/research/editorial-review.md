## Structure findings and decisions

### Accepted and resolved

- **Case-spine contract mismatch.** The outline promised one parcel through
  mutually exclusive redemption and deed outcomes, while the manuscript used
  several cases. Chapter 1 and the governing outline now declare a staged case
  portfolio: Harbour Road opens the source problem, Cedar Street carries the
  bid-to-redemption branch and Foundry Street carries the deed-and-possession
  branch. The accepted pilot files remain unchanged as historical voice
  evidence; the reviewed canonical Chapter 1 is no longer their exact
  concatenation.
- **Chapter 8 chronology.** Its opening now labels the deeded Foundry Street
  lesson as a deliberate pre-bid flash-forward. Chapter 12 enters the same deed
  branch in sequence and states its distinct ownership-work purpose.
- **Weak transfer in Chapter 13.** The repeated access case became Alder
  Crossing, a two-PID and civic-point containment conflict. The listener must
  name the verified fact, unknown, next authority and consequence before the
  narration reveals the answer.
- **Final recap dilution.** Chapter 13's durable-record section was compressed
  to a stable core and stage-change test. The applied cases and Port Hood return
  now carry the ending.
- **Opening orientation.** Chapter 1 now states the route: notice → parcel →
  context → unknowns → handoff, then bid, sale, certificate and deed stages.

## Blind beginner findings and decisions

All nine actionable findings were accepted:

- Chapter 4 removes unexplained green/red shorthand and states all four
  evidence destinations.
- Chapter 5 expands CBRM and NSPRD, date-frames the catalogue snapshot and moves
  exact build/capture particulars out of narration into the inspectable
  production record.
- Chapter 8 defers the surplus-proceeds route to Chapter 12.
- Chapter 9 separates cost groups from quote/bound/unpriceable treatments and
  defines the supported value boundary, its evidence owner and stop condition.
- Chapter 10 identifies Cedar Street as redeemable before the certificate path.
- Chapter 11 removes the spoken catalogue of nine Halifax fee amounts while
  retaining the dated, Halifax-only category lesson.
- Chapter 13 frames Meadow Line as the most research-ready evidence file, not
  the best property.

The pre-review prose gate's `hold that` match is part of “hold that priority,”
not reader management. “Here is the retrieval test” is an intentional learning
prompt. Neither was rewritten.

## Claim-traceability findings and decisions

All ten claim findings were resolved without adding new authority:

- The Chapter 1/13 Port Hood room is explicitly fictionalized; unsupported
  exact time and Inverness bidder-number mechanics were removed.
- Chapter 4 narrows the parcel-register definition to the verified Property
  Online search and content claim.
- Chapter 5 describes the July 20 map catalogue as a dated snapshot rather than
  a durable live status.
- CBRM access and resident-removal guidance is attributed to CBRM in Chapters
  6, 8, 12 and 13; other municipalities require their own current-source check.
- Chapter 10 removes the unsupported 47-PID count, proxy detail, detailed
  Pictou delivery rules and unbound CBRM payment instructions.
- Chapter 11 removes the unbound fourteen-day deadline, court-referral detail,
  exact Halifax fee amounts and certificate-of-discharge registration mechanics.
- Chapter 12 retains the supported surplus account and proportional Supreme
  Court route while removing unbound priority, interest, costs and capital-
  reserve consequences.
