# Nova Scotia municipal tax-sale result samples

These are descriptive samples from dated official publications. They do not form a valuation model, predict a future sale or prove condition, title quality, profit or later redemption status.

## Inverness County May 2025

Inverness advertised 50 properties. Official June 19, 2025 Committee of the Whole minutes say 35 sold, 15 were removed before the sale after collection or legal advice, and $608,693.21 in tax-sale surplus was collected. The separate public result sheet contains 31 bid rows, so the four-row difference remains unresolved.

For the 31 published bid rows:

- Published bids total $691,383.05; their advertised recovery amounts total $124,004.87.
- Median winning-bid/recovery ratio: 4.53; mean: 6.64.
- Range: 1.00 to 21.62.
- Five sold at approximately the recovery amount.
- Twenty-four sold for at least twice the recovery amount, 15 for at least five times, and seven for at least ten times.

The bid-minus-recovery arithmetic for these 31 rows is not the same measure as the municipality's reported tax-sale surplus, and the result sheet appears incomplete relative to the minutes. The book will preserve both official counts rather than manufacture four results.

## Inverness County August 2026 advertised inventory

The current summary advertises 45 liens: 27 redeemable and 18 non-redeemable. Advertised recovery amounts total $342,793.85, with a $3,412.09 median and a range from $879.69 to $57,875.01. Its detailed portion has 44 property sheets. Lien 6 has no detail sheet, and the amounts for liens 9 and 12 differ between the summary and detail pages. These differences are reasons to confirm the live list and amounts with the municipality, not evidence of parcel defects.

## CBRM March 2026

Source: CBRM's official March 10, 2026 sold-property PDF, snapshot SHA-256 `ed0dbc1dcc09a7fb9a063b716784cb1b3ba18306f9984013791e4b250f667dc4`.

Method: extract rows containing both a minimum bid and a winning bid from the official PDF text. Twenty-four recorded sales met that condition.

- Median winning-bid/minimum-bid ratio: 3.17.
- Range: 1.00 to 42.25.
- Eight of 24 sold exactly at the minimum.
- Nine of 24 sold for at least five times the minimum.
- Illustrative anonymized pairs: $970.37 to $41,000; $20,730.48 to $195,000; $1,085.94 to $9,500; $7,132.30 to $58,000.

Interpretation boundary: this is one municipality and one auction, not a market forecast. It shows why the minimum bid is a debt-recovery number rather than a valuation signal. It does not establish market value, building condition, title quality, profit, or the later redemption status of any parcel.

## Richmond County June 2026

Richmond published three sold rows. The listed charges and bids were $2,633.57 to $3,500, $4,400 to $29,000 and $4,933.60 to $31,000—approximately 1.33, 6.59 and 6.28 times the recovery amounts. This tiny sample is useful only as evidence that competition varies even within a single event.
