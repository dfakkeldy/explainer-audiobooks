# Candidate 2 — The Raised Card

This is the bright, auction-forward direction. A blank bidder card casts a
parcel-shaped shadow that stops at a small magenta unknown, reframing bidding as
the beginning of research rather than the end of it.

- Source art: generated with the built-in image-generation tool from the
  approved no-text/no-logo direction in `../cover-directions.md`.
- Typography: bundled geometric sans, condensed display and technical mono.
- Palette: pale sky, charcoal, coral-red and electric teal.
- Rights/provenance: original generated source art; no external photograph,
  identifiable person, real auction venue, address or documentary claim.
- Review note: the renderer's subtitle contrast advisory was inspected at full
  size and thumbnail size. The final scrim fades across the full canvas without
  a visible rectangular edge.

The portrait and square specifications share the exact source-art SHA-256
recorded in their render receipts. The pair must be selected together.
