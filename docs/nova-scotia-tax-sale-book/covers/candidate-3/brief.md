# Candidate 3 — Layers of Uncertainty

This is the map-led thesis direction. Torn paper maps, a folded notice and an
open magenta seam embody notice → parcel → context → unknowns → handoff without
pretending that mapped evidence settles the property.

- Source art: generated with the built-in image-generation tool from the
  approved no-text/no-logo direction in `../cover-directions.md`.
- Typography: bundled geometric sans, condensed display, editorial serif and
  technical mono.
- Palette: midnight blue, sea teal, warm off-white and vivid magenta.
- Rights/provenance: original generated source art; no external photograph,
  live parcel data, owner information, crest, map UI or documentary claim.
- Review note: the renderer's subtitle contrast advisory was inspected at full
  size and thumbnail size. The final dark scrim fades across the full canvas
  without a visible rectangular edge.

The portrait and square specifications share the exact source-art SHA-256
recorded in their render receipts. The pair must be selected together.
