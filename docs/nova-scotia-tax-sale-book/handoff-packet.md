## Core promise

The listener can explain the legal sequence, read municipal terminology, build
a bounded evidence file, recognize the auction and certificate-holder failure
paths, and identify the questions that belong with a lawyer, surveyor, planner,
insurer, accountant or other qualified professional.

## Opening orientation

- Context: Inverness County already publishes a substantial parcel packet.
- Promise: show what it establishes, what remains unknown and what additional
  source-traceable research can responsibly add.
- Route: travel backward from the packet to lien and notice, then forward
  through auction or tender, payment, certificate, possible redemption, deed,
  contestability and post-deed responsibility.

## Boundaries

Educational only; no legal, tax, insurance, investment, appraisal, survey,
access, environmental, planning, tenancy or construction advice. No property
recommendation, personalized bid, trespass instruction, owner narrative or
promise that a certificate interest can be insured. Halifax must be taught
under the HRM Charter parallel rather than silently folded into the MGA.
