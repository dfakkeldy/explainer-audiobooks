## Chapter 13 · Putting the Method Together {#ans-13}

### 13.1 {#ans-13-1}

The verified fact is that the dated notice lists one account with two PIDs and that the returned
civic point is contained in one of the two graphical parcel polygons. The unresolved question is what
interest the municipality intends to sell and how the two PIDs, account, legal descriptions and any
building relate. The next authorities are the municipality's current notice or confirmation and the
governed land-record review interpreted by counsel. The consequence is a stop. No parcel may be
silently dropped from the file merely because the civic point and roof make one shape more
memorable: civic-point containment found a parcel to investigate; it did not rewrite a two-PID notice
into a one-parcel sale. Until the municipal record and land-record evidence reconcile the two PIDs,
the file cannot support a title request, use analysis or maximum bid. See [Three parcels, three
different endings](#sec-13-three-endings).

### 13.2 {#ans-13-2}

The building image does not identify who is present, the person's legal status or whether vacant
possession can be obtained. The historical business reference does not diagnose contamination. The
negative registry search means only that this search found no matching record in that source; it
does not certify the soil, water, air, building materials or contents. The assessed-value record
records a dated mass-appraisal and taxation context, including the assessment classification shown
by the public source; it is not a current appraisal of the occupied building, an inspection of the
former use or a prediction of the auction result. Nothing in the fictional notice supplies resident
removal or entry. See [Three parcels, three different endings](#sec-13-three-endings).

### 13.3 {#ans-13-3}

The researcher does not invent reserves large enough to make the unknowns disappear. With no lawful
inspection opportunity, unresolved occupancy status and no time for an environmental professional to
review the former-use evidence, the unknowns are not yet priceable. The file records why they are not
yet priceable and which facts would be required to reopen it. Occupancy belongs in a lawyer-reviewed
possession and tenancy file; former use and current condition belong in a property-specific
environmental and building review; an insurer can respond to the actual use, occupancy and condition
evidence; tax treatment needs its own route. The bidder owns the decision to stop because essential
information cannot be obtained in time. See [Three parcels,
three different endings](#sec-13-three-endings).

### 13.4 {#ans-13-4}

The graphical boundary remains an orientation record rather than a survey. The road relationship
supports a focused access question rather than a legal right. The zoning response identifies current
zoning and applicable provisions; it is not a permit, service confirmation or promise that the
intended project will be approved. Empty environmental, well, sewage, coastal or mine searches retain
the coverage and currency limits of their sources. The routes: the lawyer can review the parcel
register, legal description and access evidence; the municipality can answer the exact frontage,
lot, intended-use and approval questions; service and site professionals can test what the public
record cannot; a surveyor can be engaged if the boundary or road relationship matters to the plan.
The file does not need every possible fact; it needs every decision-critical uncertainty either
resolved or assigned a defensible treatment. See [Three parcels, three different
endings](#sec-13-three-endings).

### 13.5 {#ans-13-5}

Payment readiness remains a separate gate. A parcel can fit the evidence and budget while the bidder
lacks the authority, accepted funds or balance path required by the current event terms; that file is
not ready. The maximum must also be written: the supported value boundary and the all-in costs
produce it, and auction energy cannot raise it. Even when the evidence may support participation under
a fixed maximum and verified payment plan, the researcher can explain why the file reached that point
but still cannot declare the parcel a bargain, recommend a bid or decide that the bidder should
proceed. See [Three parcels, three different endings](#sec-13-three-endings).

### 13.6 {#ans-13-6}

The file opens the certificate-holder records and stops speaking about a possible bid. The new stage
record states the controlling document, the power or duty it creates, its boundary, the next
authorized action and the event that will end that stage; the old evidence remains dated evidence of
what was known and does not continue to authorize action. Full redemption payment to the treasurer
ends the purchaser's rights. If the sale is not redeemed, it can proceed to the deed request and a new
ownership file when the applicable wait and municipal process permit it. The registered deed has real
statutory effect, but it still does not make the old map a survey, remove occupants, inspect the
building, create a right-of-way or approve development. See [The record that survives the
event](#sec-13-record).
