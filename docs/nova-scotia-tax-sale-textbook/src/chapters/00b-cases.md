# The Cases in This Book

Every case below is a fictional composite: not a real property, not a real person, and never a
recommendation. No single fictional parcel can demonstrate every branch of the process without
contradiction, so the book uses a small portfolio of named cases, each tested under different facts.

| Case | Chapters | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Harbour Road | 1, 2, 3 | A municipal page that gives a parcel an account number, a parcel identifier, a mapped shape and assessment information, and the questions that page cannot answer; later, a paid purchase that becomes a registered certificate and a protective roof repair during redemption. |
| Quarry Lane | 3 | A parcel in the older-arrears category, called at the same auction as Harbour Road, which follows the route without the normal six-month redemption. |
| Birch Point Road | 4, 5 | A packet entry turned into a parcel biography, then researched on a public map from notice to handoff. |
| Maple Ridge | 6 | A narrow sliver behind two roadside lots, where a visible track is not a right of access and a permitted use is not an approved project. |
| Breakwater Lane | 7 | A cove-side parcel with a possible former workshop, where map layers and an empty search become records questions rather than findings. |
| Foundry Street | 8, 12 | A deeded property with a registered tax deed and an occupied building, followed after the deed through title, possession, a right-of-way and a revised plan. |
| Harbour Park | 8 | A listing that uses a mobile home identifier while the map selects the underlying land, so land and home records may tell different stories. |
| Cedar Street, with Maya | 9, 10, 11 | A building with a four-figure recovery amount and a six-figure assessment, priced backward from a defined use; Maya, a fictional bidder, rehearses, bids, pays and then lives through the certificate-holder months. |
| Elena | 12 | A fictional former co-owner whose interest may have attached before the sale, and who follows the surplus route after it. |
| Alder Crossing | 13 | One account and two parcel identifiers, where an address search and an aerial image point at only one of the parcels. |
| Union Workshop | 13 | An apparently occupied building with older directory references to repair work, two days before the sale. |
| Meadow Line | 13 | A file whose records agree, which still names every confirmation it needs before a decision. |

The figure plates labelled Parcel A, B and C come from the 2026 edition and keep their labels:

- **Parcel A:** plates 1–4 in Chapter 6, used for Maple Ridge, and plate 5 in Chapter 7, used for the
  Breakwater Lane discussion.
- **Parcel B:** plates 1, 3 and 5 in Chapter 7, and plates 2 and 4 in Chapter 8.
- **Parcel C:** plates 1–5 in Chapter 13, used for the Meadow Line discussion.
