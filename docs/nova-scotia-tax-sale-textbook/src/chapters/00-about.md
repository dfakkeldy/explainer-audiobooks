# About this book

This is the textbook edition of *Beyond the Tax-Sale Packet: How Nova Scotia Municipal Auctions
Really Work*, by Dan Fakkeldy. It was rewritten for the page and designed with Claude Opus 5.5
(Anthropic) from the 2026 governed-final edition, which was written, researched, reviewed and
produced with OpenAI Codex (GPT-5), using official Nova Scotia and municipal sources.

The two editions teach the same material in the same thirteen chapters, in the same order. The
2026 edition was written for the ear; this one is written for a reader with a pencil. It adds the
apparatus of a textbook: learning objectives, worked cases, file notes, source notes, summaries,
questions with answers, and diagrams drawn for the page.

::: {.rail label="Educational only"}
The material is educational only. It is not legal, tax, investment, title, surveying, appraisal,
access, environmental, insurance, planning, tenancy or construction advice. Municipal lists and
procedures change. Always verify the current municipal notice, statute and event terms, and use
qualified professionals for a live property decision.
:::

::: {.rail label="How facts are dated"}
Facts as of July 22, 2026. The facts are those of the 2026 edition, and the dates its sources were
checked travel with them. Facts about a particular event, such as a notice, a sale date, a
registration amount or a result, are dated examples of how one municipality ran one event. They are
not rules for every sale.
:::

::: {.rail label="Composite cases"}
Every named parcel and person in the worked cases is a fictional composite, not a real property or
a real person. *The cases in this book*, after the contents, lists them with the chapters where each one
appears. The book prints no owner or occupant names.
:::

## How to use this book

Each chapter opens with the same apparatus, so a reader who dips into one chapter can find their
footing:

- **Research chain.** Five links, notice → parcel → context → unknowns → handoff, with the links the
  chapter develops filled in.
- **You will learn to.** What you should be able to do after the chapter.
- **Key terms.** The words the chapter defines.

Inside the chapter, labels in the left margin name what a passage is doing. Shaded boxes carry the
worked cases. Index cards headed **File note** show a researcher's bounded notes, with the source,
date, observation, limitation, unknown and next authority kept together. Panels marked **Careful**
mark the places where a reader is most likely to overread a record. Short notes headed **Where this
comes from** close each section with its official sources.

Each chapter ends with a summary and a few questions under **Check your understanding**. Each
question links to its answer in Appendix A. Appendix B is a blank worksheet for sorting a file into
what is known, what is unresolved and what belongs to a professional. Appendix C indexes the statutes
and official sources by chapter, and Appendix D lists every figure with its credit.

The footer of every page spells out the abbreviations on that page and defines the terms used on it.
The glossary near the end collects them all.
