# What changed: textbook edition versus the 2026 governed-final edition

A summary for review. The 2026 edition (`books/beyond-the-tax-sale-packet/`) and its packet
(`docs/nova-scotia-tax-sale-book/`) are unchanged. Per-chapter detail is in `review/changes/chNN.md`; the
claim-by-claim mapping is in `review/claims/chNN.md`; doubts about the original are in
`review/flagged-claims.md` (kept as printed, never silently fixed).

## Whole book

- Same 13 chapters in the same order. Each chapter has a new descriptive title; the original title
  is kept as its kicker (for example "Chapter 6 · The driveway that may not be a right-of-way").
- Written for the page instead of the ear: audio framing ("road listener", "after an interruption",
  "Return to…") removed; rhetorical questions turned into statements or lists; spelled-out numbers
  set as numerals; runs of "X does not establish Y" set as lists and two-column tables.
- Every qualifier (may, can, appears, about, subject to…) kept. Dated event facts framed "as of July
  2026"; no fact refreshed (facts as of July 22, 2026).
- About 66,000 words in the book (the original narrates 32,983), the difference being apparatus,
  case boxes, source notes, answers and back matter.

## Language, by chapter

- **1. How a Property Reaches a Tax Sale.** Audio framing removed ("Imagine", "Return to…", "Here is the retrieval test"). Rhetorical questions became statements, except the eight genuine research questions, now a list. Numbers are numerals (45, 44, lien 6, 14, 60, 30, June 30). The five "X is not Y" limits became a two-column table. Qualifiers (may, perhaps, often, sometimes, subject to) are unchanged.
- **2. Reading the Notice.** Audio framing removed ("Return to…", "A useful way to test the file is to interrupt it", "the listener" → "a reader"). The auction-morning question became a statement plus four numbered steps. Numbers are numerals (45, 44, lien 6, 27/18, $4,000, July 20, August 10/11). Comma-chained limit lists became bullet lists; the closing six-field recap became a table. Qualifiers (may, normally, can, if that confirmation actually occurred) are unchanged.
- **3. Certificate, Redemption and Deed.** Audio framing removed ("For a road listener", "Now interrupt the story", "Return once more to…", "If the listener can narrate"). Runs of "X does not Y" became lists and tables: the powers and their boundaries, the six things the deed does not do, and the document sequence. Numbers are numerals (10 percent). Qualifiers (may, can, most, normal, where required) are unchanged.
- **4. Building the Parcel File.** The audio framing ("after an interruption") is gone. Run-on series became lists: packet contents, inventory questions, source-ledger lines, the register's limits, the bounded observations, the lawyer's questions and the file sent to the lawyer. Positive statements now come before their limits ("Its job is to state what the instrument describes, not…"). Qualifiers (may, might, can, appears, merely) are unchanged.
- **5. Using the Map as a Research Tool.** Audio framing removed ("road listener", "when this passage is heard", "In narration", "can be spoken", "Now imagine an interruption", "Return to the interrupted listener"). Runs of "does not prove…" became lists and tables (three record-family errors, containment, the water result, layer limits, handoffs). Numbers are numerals (14 parcels; 1-percent or 5-percent). Qualifiers (may, about, approximate, by itself, checked) are unchanged.
- **6. Access and Intended Use.** Audio rhetoric is trimmed ("Stand conceptually" became a plain instruction); the original's run-on series became lists (what the image cannot show, what title research must ask, the zoning questions, what the planner's call names). The run of denials at `md:801` and the permitted-use limits became two-column tables. Every qualifier (may, might, appears, can, still) and every File Note's wording are unchanged.
- **7. Physical and Environmental Records.** Audio framing ("after an interruption") removed. Run-on series became lists: the workshop's questions, what an empty search does not establish, what a well log does not guarantee, the sewage result's possible realities, the mine record's limits, when uncertainty governs the bid. Qualifiers (may, can, appears, roughly, suggests) unchanged; "fifty" printed as 50.
- **8. Title and Possession.** Audio framing is gone ("After an interruption", "listener" became "bidder"); the original's long series became lists (what the title file needs, the court order's subjects, the title-quality questions, what counsel may need to determine, the four "No…" restraints). The two statements about "clear title" and the closing hand-off of authorities became two-column tables. Every qualifier (may, can, as the Act specifies) and both File Notes' wording are unchanged.
- **9. Results, Costs and the Maximum Bid.** Audio wording is removed ("After an interruption", "road-book arithmetic", "the concrete reset", "listener" → researcher/bidder). Spelled-out numbers became numerals. Long series became lists (what a sheet does not record, what travels with a comparison, the worksheet's contents). Runs of denials became tables (historical bid; what evidence cannot generate; reserve examples). Every qualifier (may, might, about, approximately, currently, can) is unchanged.
- **10. Auction Day, Tenders and Payment.** Audio framing removed ("retrieval under noise" became a Quick recall; "listener" → "reader"). Run-on series became lists: temptations, prohibited buyers, payment forms, payment-sheet fields, the bid sequence, the five-part "ready means" checklist, state changes, outcome names. "Forty-five" → 45, "two-hundred-dollar" → $200. Present-tense CBRM statements dated July 2026. Qualifiers (may, can, automatically, forthwith) unchanged.
- **11. The Certificate-Holder Months.** Audio phrasing removed or neutralized ("The concrete reset" → "The concrete test"; "spoken as though" → "described as though"); "ten percent" → "10 percent"; stray spaces in "six- month" and "fire- insurance" removed. Long series became lists (powers and limits, the operating file, the insurer inquiry, redemption categories, notices at close-out, the final ledger). Denial runs became tables (insurance stages; active restraint; where each category stays). Every qualifier (may, can, certain, qualifying, if insurable) is unchanged; "Current" insurer materials are dated "(checked July 2026)".
- **12. After the Deed.** Audio framing removed ("Now enter that branch", "That distinction blocks a dangerous shortcut"). Present-tense official statements dated "as of July 2026". Spelled-out numbers became numerals ("twenty years" → "20 years"). Long series became lists (deed checks, registration evidence, Marketable Titles Act qualifications, decision record); runs of denials became tables (three records; what each answer does not supply; three files). Every qualifier (may, generally, can, currently, subject to) is unchanged.
- **13. Putting the Method Together.** the audio recap and rhythm became scannable structure. Each file's narrative moved into composite case boxes; the rules stay in the running text. The four-part sentences became tables, and so did the runs of "X does not establish Y" (clue → limit; record → supports → limit) and the map's can/should-not list. The stable core, stage record, roles and outcomes are lists or tables. "Declines both" now names resident removal and entry; two hyphenation artifacts are closed up (F-13a). Pointers to Chapters 9 and 10 were added. No claim was dropped or added (202-row ledger).

## Apparatus added

- Chapter openings: research-chain strip, learning objectives (13 sets) and key terms.
- 54 worked-case boxes for the composite cases, each labelled as a composite; 14 File Notes (the original's bounded research notes as index cards).
- 34 side-head passages, 10 Careful panels, 11 Quick recall boxes (from the original's audio recovery passages).
- 39 source notes ("Where this comes from") citing the packet's source register and evidence notes.
- 13 chapter summaries and 70 Check your understanding questions, answered in Appendix A.
- Front matter: About this book (credits, verbatim disclaimer, dating, licence, Province attribution, how to use the book) and The Cases in This Book.
- Back matter: Appendix A answers; Appendix B blank known/unresolved/professional worksheet; Appendix C index of statute provisions and official sources by chapter and section; Appendix D list of figures with credits; glossary (per-page footers in the PDF, chapter notes in the EPUB); Sources and Credits.
