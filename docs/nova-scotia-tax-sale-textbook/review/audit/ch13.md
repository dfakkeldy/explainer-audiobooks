# Audit — Chapter 13

Method: I read the original (`books/beyond-the-tax-sale-packet/beyond-the-tax-sale-packet.md`
md:1497–1613) cold first. I listed about 150 factual, legal and date claims and qualifiers, for
example "None represents a live tax-sale property", "may once have been", "authoritative civic
point", "or confirmation", "interpreted by counsel", "from a lawful public position", "only that
this search found no matching record in that source", "If the event terms state", "not yet
priceable", "most research-ready … not a property ranking", "often exceeded", "not identical counts
for every measure", "genuinely priceable", "may support participation", "Full redemption payment to
the treasurer", "when the applicable wait and municipal process permit it", "where it can", "when
their year and type are unmistakable" and "unless reuse is authorized". I then checked every
sentence of `src/chapters/13-putting-the-method-together.md`: front matter, chain, objectives, key
terms, all tables and lists, case boxes, rail, both source notes, summary, the six check questions
and all seven captions and alt texts. I also checked the six answers in `src/appendix-answers/ch13.md`.
For the 7 figures, I read the visible `<text>` in each `fig-13-*.svg` and compared it with the
original alts, the ledger's transcription of the PNG plate text (bands, cards and stamps) and
`figures/ch13_figures.py`. I checked the source-note citations against `research/evidence-notes.md`
(DATA-003, DATA-005, LAND-001 to LAND-006, MAP-001 to MAP-003, MAP-007, OCC-001, TAX-003, ENV-001,
LAW-007, LAW-009, LAW-011 and LAW-012) and against `research/sources.md` (nos. 1, 2, 7, 10, 15, 17,
20, 25, 28, 38 and 40). All of them support the text they are attached to. Every number, date and
section reference matched. I found no changed legal meaning.

The problems were wording drift. A dropped "or confirmation" in the new figure. Union Workshop's
unknowns restated as "cannot be answered before the sale" rather than "cannot be responsibly
investigated before the event". Ranking-like "strongest file" wording in the summary. Two small
editorial assertions.

Privacy: the chapter has no owner names, PIDs, AANs or civic addresses. Port Hood, Harbour Road and
the three fictional files all appear in the original. The figure identifiers are masked ("••••").
The worksheet cross-reference ("a blank copy is in Appendix B") is correct: `98-worksheet.md` is
Appendix B. The ledger's "Appendix C" note is stale.
