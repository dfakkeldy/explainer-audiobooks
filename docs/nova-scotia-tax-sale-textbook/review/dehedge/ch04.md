- "a bidder may pay first for the question…": a permissive "may" (an option), not a hedge.
- "if it arrives at all": audit-checked qualifier.
- "Surveyor/lawyer/planner can…" (A04-03) and "can support one another without becoming interchangeable" (F-26): audit-kept or flagged, and left as printed.
