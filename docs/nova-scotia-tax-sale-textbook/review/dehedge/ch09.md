Most of this chapter's qualifiers carry real conditions. Tax treatment depends on the buyer, the
property and the transaction. The federal end date is perishable. The reserve table sets out
case-by-case outcomes. The cuts are the caption boilerplate and a handful of "can"/"may" softeners
on things the records or the cost structure plainly do.

   without being perfectly interchangeable fields": uncertain. The fields differ by municipality.
   This is the comparison lesson and check question 3.
2. "Descriptive samples can challenge folklore while remaining unsuitable for prediction": a real
   limit on what the samples can do, not a softener.
3. Iceberg rail and Figure 9.4 caption "can sit below the waterline/surface": audit-restored
   (A09-05). The layers vary by file.
4. Figure 9.2 caption "often carried bids above": audit kept it (A09-07) to match the printed figure.
5. "Insurance may be available only on particular terms, or may remain unresolved…": uncertain. It
   depends on the insurer and the building.
6. Reserve table "Might support a reserve when…", "May remain a no-go", "May be unpriceable when…":
   source conditional (md:1123). Each depends on the evidence in the case, and the answer to 9.4
   matches.
7. Non-resident tax "can apply to qualifying non-residents", "subject to the governing definitions
   and exemptions", and "The assessment can matter to this tax calculation": source conditional
   (TAX-002). Applying the tax and using the assessed value both depend on status and on price
   against assessment.
8. "A bidder should establish status and treatment with current provincial guidance and
   professional advice before the auction" and "obtain case-specific tax advice": specific
   handoffs on individual tax questions (method, not disclaimer). One per branch.
9. HST "can depend on the seller, prior use, property, transaction, and purchaser's registration
   status" and "not a safe universal sentence": source conditional (TAX-003; F-09b kept as printed;
   A09-02 "not a universal one").
10. "currently scheduled through January 1, 2027" and "Those categories require exact current
    review": perishable and uncertain (F-09a, ELIG-001). The A09-01 alt text stays as audited.
11. "An unanswered branch does not automatically mean no bid…" (text, caption, summary):
    audit-restored (A09-04).
12. "What remains can become the maximum bid if every essential … condition is satisfied" and "when
    the stakes or uncertainty require it": source conditional. The maximum stands only if the
    conditions hold.
13. "Current municipal terms control the accepted methods and event details": varies by
    municipality and event (LAW-006).
14. "far too few to support a municipal price rule", "the blocks do not represent exact row
    positions", "approximately the recovery amount": precise limits from the audit/source, not
    softeners.
