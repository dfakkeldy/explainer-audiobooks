## Kept hedged

- "an occupied building that may once have been a workshop": genuinely uncertain. Older directories
  only associate the address with repair work. The audit lists it as a qualifier.
- "Where CBRM serves as the public comparison … the real event still requires a current-source check":
  this varies by municipality and event (OCC-001). It is the chapter's only such reminder.
- "If the event terms state that HST applies … The actual federal treatment still depends on the
  seller, property, use and transaction": the source is conditional (TAX-003), and the audit lists it.
- "published bids often exceeded recovery amounts" and "not identical counts for every measure": the
  audit lists both, and the source is a past-event sample (DATA-003).
- "The evidence may support participation" (Meadow Line decision point; answer 13.5 "may support
  participation"): this is a real condition, and the audit lists it. The researcher cannot say the
  bidder should proceed.
- "If the essential legal and use questions return acceptable answers" and "keeps a reserve for
  uncertainty that is genuinely priceable": the source is conditional, and the audit lists
  "genuinely priceable".
- "when the applicable wait and municipal process permit it" and "where it can" (post-deed file):
  both conditional, and the audit lists them (LAW-012).
- "unless reuse is authorized" and "when their year and type are unmistakable": real conditions,
  and the audit lists them.
- "cannot be responsibly investigated before the event" (alt, list, summary): an audit fix (A13-02,
  A13-03). Left as restored.
- "The municipality's current notice or confirmation" and "interpreted by counsel": audit-restored
  (A13-01), and listed qualifiers.
- "no parcel may be silently dropped": a rule, not a hedge.
- "A surveyor can be engaged if the boundary or road relationship matters to the plan": a real
  condition.
- "Meadow Line is the most research-ready … It is not a property ranking" and the summary's
  restatement: these follow the style guide's ban on ranking language and F-13d (A13-04). The
  summary is a recap.
- "A documented decision not to proceed can be equally complete": this is the chapter's point, not
  uncertainty. Both the original and the summary say it.
- Flags F-13a to F-13d, including the quoted "strongest file" band in the Figure 13.2 caption:
  untouched.
