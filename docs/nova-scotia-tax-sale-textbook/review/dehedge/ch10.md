# De-hedge — Chapter 10

Chapter 10 was already fairly direct. Most of its "may" is statutory and permissive. Examples are the treasurer's power to call tenders with council's consent, council's power to authorize another legal-tender method, the treasurer's power to bid for the municipality and the municipality's power to advertise again. All of those stay. This pass removed softeners on plain statements, a redundant variability sentence, and the "verify / educational overview / not a recommendation" disclaimers repeated in figure captions.

Hedges removed: 14 (chapter 12, answers 2)

Chapter (12):
- §10.1 CBRM paragraph: deleted the redundant sentence "Inverness, CBRM and any other municipality can publish different operational details within the governing law." The preceding sentence already says these are CBRM's instructions and not rules for every sale (1).
- Opposite temptations list: "the bidder may chase" / "may raise" changed to "the temptation is to chase" / "to raise" (2).
- Figure 10.1 caption: deleted "Verify sources; not a recommendation." (1).
- One durable rule: "may feel entirely different" changed to "feel entirely different" (1).
- Figure 10.2 caption: deleted "• verify current event terms. Educational overview • verify current law and sale terms." The source citation stays (2).
- Finish-line rail: removed the throat-clearing "The finish-line analogy helps as long as it remains limited." The limit paragraph stays (1).
- Payment-failure case: "The evidence file may be sound and the price may be authorized" changed to "is sound … is authorized". This is the stipulation inside a "Suppose" scenario (2).
- State changes: "They are better understood as state changes" changed to "They are state changes" (1).
- Figure 10.3 caption: deleted the repeated "another municipality's next step must be checked in its own current record". The body says it once in §10.3 (1).

Answers (2):
- 10.1: "the bidder may chase / may raise" changed to "the temptation is to chase / to raise", to match the chapter (2).

## Kept hedged

- "Those are CBRM's event instructions, not unwritten rules for every Nova Scotia sale" and the summary's "not rules for every Nova Scotia sale": these terms vary by municipality and event. The point is stated once in the body and once in the summary.
- "a municipality may use more than one statutory route over time" (Annapolis): permissive, and limited to what the source shows.
- "possible loss of office or employment": the penalty depends on the case, and the audit lists it among the qualifiers it checked.
- "any listed account may have been paid, removed or otherwise changed": genuinely uncertain on the day (OPS-003).
- "Event-specific fees or payment instructions can sit beside that obligation": varies by municipality.
- "A bank draft … is not automatically adjustable" and "The next person does not automatically inherit…": the audit kept "automatically" on purpose.
- "'May' matters" and the treasurer's and municipality's "may" in the no-sufficient-bid branch: these grant a power and are not uncertain.
- "CBRM's … legend … an unsold property may be purchased only at a future tax sale" and "Another municipality's next step must be checked in its own current record" (body, once): CBRM's dated wording, and the rule varies by municipality.
- "The amount and timing of that result depend on the actual municipal process; the book cannot calculate them": genuinely conditional, and check question 5 asks about it.
- "A municipal tender notice may also describe how acceptance, rejection or movement to another bid is administered": varies by event.
- "The reviewed tax-sale provisions do not supply a general bidder-facing script…": this is the evidence-note boundary (OPS-006).
- "seeks legal advice if the change itself is disputed": a specific handoff to a professional, not a disclaimer.
- During-event and after-offer "may" in the state-changes list: these are real alternative outcomes.
- The Figure 10.2 caption's "The Inverness registration amount is that event's term, not a provincial rule": varies by event.
- Flags F-10a to F-10d and audit fixes A10-01 to A10-04 are untouched. That includes the source note's "the evidence notes do not itemize the list", the summary's "beneficially owns … at that municipality's tax sale" and answer 10.3's council-power sentence.

Summary: chapter 10 removed=14 kept=15
