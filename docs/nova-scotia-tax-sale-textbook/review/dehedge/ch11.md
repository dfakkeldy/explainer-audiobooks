## Kept hedged

1. "the tax deed that may follow after six months if nobody redeems" (body and summary): audit
   qualifier. The deed depends on no redemption and on a request.
2. "an ending that may return the property to someone else" (intro and body): redemption is uncertain.
3. "merely because the purchase money was paid": audit qualifier.
4. "ordinary home coverage may not continue to handle … may be required": audit qualifiers. Market
   illustration only (INS-001, F-11a).
5. "the redemption amount may include … necessary repairs … when … written approval" (body, summary
   and answer 11.4): audit-restored wording (A11-01, A11-02).
6. "The formula can also include: certain older unpaid taxes when …; qualifying fire-insurance
   premiums" and summary "can add certain …": audit-restored. The items are conditional.
7. "the purchaser may be asked for a statement": audit qualifier. The statement is given on request.
8. "When any of those questions is unsettled …" and "each unsettled question" (chain strip, summary):
   audit-restored (A11-03, A11-06).
9. "may use the statutory route within the applicable six-month period", "the holder may collect
   rents / may use the land", "why Maya may insure": these grant powers, so they aren't hedges.
10. "A former owner may be trying to preserve …; an occupant may not be the person arranging
    payment": these really are unknown.
11. "Cedar Street's municipality may use different council-approved fees": varies by municipality.
    The same goes for "Halifax's categories … can change after this edition is drafted" (a dated
    snapshot).
12. "A policy may have its own cancellation or change terms"; "An approved contractor may need a stop
    instruction"; "if any were lawfully created": these depend on the case.
13. "a loose roof covering that could admit water"; "A missed notice can create a practical problem":
    these are real risks, not settled outcomes.
14. Counsel/professional handoffs (entry and possession, the insurer's refusal, disputed payments):
    each hands off one specific question. That is method, not a disclaimer, so all were kept.
15. Figure 11.1 caption "Verify sources; not a recommendation.": kept. The same footer appears on
    every figure redrawn from a 2026 plate (Chapters 12–13), so it looks like the original's plate
    language (style guide §7: disclaimer language intact where the original has it). The coordinator
    can remove it across all the redrawn figures if it isn't the original's.
