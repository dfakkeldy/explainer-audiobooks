## Kept hedged

- "a tax deed generally may be set aside only during the six years following its registration" (§12.1,
  §12.3, summary, Figure 12.1 alt, answer 12.3): this is LAW-018's own wording. "Generally" carries
  the statutory exceptions. (source conditional)
- "the preserved possibility of a damages claim": LAW-018 preserves "any cause of action a person
  may have". (source conditional)
- "The page does not turn automatically on the sixth-month anniversary": the qualifier is the
  content. (audit-protected wording)
- Permissive "may": "the purchaser may request the deed after the sale", "may apply to the Supreme
  Court", "The court may order proportional payment", "A claimant may allege". (statutory power,
  not a hedge)
- "Those amounts can change" / "registry fees can change" (Land Registry fees): F-18, perishable.
  (varies over time)
- "Foundry Street's actual municipality requires its own current-source check": CBRM is only the
  example. (varies by municipality)
- "Cape Breton Regional Municipality, for example": the audit listed "for example" as a qualifier.
  (audit-protected / varies by municipality)
- "The taxable assessed value may differ, including through the Capped Assessment Program.
  Transfer-related eligibility can also change.": F-12b, kept as printed. (flagged)
- "A policy or binder … may have terms tied to that conditional interest": depends on the actual
  policy. (uncertain, case-specific)
- "where another parcel may have passage rights": the owner doesn't know the route and extent until
  reading the instrument. (uncertain)
- "A selected parcel sheet may carry that dated PVSC account": not every sheet does. (conditional)
- "A building professional may find … may change the work sequence": hypothetical inspection
  outcomes. (uncertain)
- "A plan … may fail once those systems return real costs": an outcome that isn't known yet.
  (uncertain)
- "Elena's interest may have attached to the property": her claim is unproven. That is the point of
  the case. (uncertain)
- "a court may be the decision-maker"; "possible share"; "possible proceeds claim"; "if one
  appears": real case-by-case uncertainty. (uncertain)
- "contamination can affect soil, water and air": ENV-001 wording. (source)
- "The figure shows when each clock starts, not how any claim will turn out" (Figure 12.1 caption)
  and the Careful panel's "counsel handles the actual facts and remedies": the one clear handoff on
  timing and exceptions. (kept once, at the place it matters)
- "after the redemption period" for the surplus application: F-17, kept as printed. (flagged)
