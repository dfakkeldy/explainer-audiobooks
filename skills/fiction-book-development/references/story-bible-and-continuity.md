# Story Bible and Continuity

The bible is a compact decision record for the lead writer. It is not an
encyclopedia and it never outranks accepted manuscript text. When discovery in
the prose changes canon, reconcile the bible immediately.

## Story contract

Record:

- form, audience, target length, genre and subgenre;
- one-sentence dramatic premise;
- reader promise: expected tension, emotion, wonder, humour, mystery, or catharsis;
- central opposition and consequence of failure;
- thematic question, phrased as a tension rather than a moral answer;
- POV, tense, narrative distance, chronology, and structural conventions;
- ending direction and the change that can make it earned;
- content boundaries, privacy, rights, and research requirements.

A genre promise governs reader experience, not a checklist of borrowed tropes.
Name which conventions will be fulfilled, complicated, or deliberately refused.

## Character engine

For each principal character record only story-generating facts:

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Public desire | What they actively pursue and can articulate |
| Buried need | What change or recognition the story pressures |
| Fear / avoidance | What costly action they resist |
| Contradiction | Two credible impulses that cannot remain comfortable together |
| Leverage | What they can use against the problem or another person |
| Limit | What prevents easy victory |
| Misbelief | A working interpretation the story may test, not a slogan |
| Voice | Rhythm, diction, evasions, humour, metaphors, taboo words |
| Relationship pressure | What they want from each principal relationship |
| Change range | Plausible transformation, refusal, corruption, or revelation |

Backstory belongs only when it changes present action, interpretation, or risk.
Define antagonistic force by its credible objective and method; opposition should
not exist merely to obstruct the protagonist.

## World constraints

Record rules that affect choice and consequence:

- physical, social, political, economic, technological, or magical constraints;
- who knows each rule and how they learned it;
- cost, limit, exception, enforcement, and likely exploit;
- place-specific sensory anchors and social behaviour;
- research status for real cultures, professions, places, trauma, or history.

Do not draft decorative lore as a substitute for conflict. If a rule never
constrains a scene or shapes meaning, it can remain undefined.

## Research and representation ledger

Separate invented story facts from checkable real-world claims. Track each
consequential question with its narrative consequence, source or consultation,
confidence, representation risk, targeted reader or expert needed, and final
disposition. Research details that materially affect behaviour, causality,
language, institutions, geography, history, disability, medicine, law, trauma,
or cultural practice.

Do not fabricate citations, lived experience, dialect, or community consensus.
Do not use a marginalized identity only as plot machinery or exotic texture.
Targeted readers identify effects and blind spots; they do not replace research,
grant permission, or assume final author responsibility. Flag unresolved
high-impact uncertainty instead of converting it into authoritative prose, and
do not copy copyrighted source prose into project artifacts.

## Turn architecture

For each major turn record:

1. the prior stable assumption or plan;
2. the choice, revelation, loss, or intrusion that breaks it;
3. why the event follows from established motive and circumstance;
4. the new options and closed options;
5. the emotional and relationship consequence;
6. the promise created for later payoff.

The outline may change, but the causal chain must remain legible.

## Continuity ledgers

Update after each accepted chapter. Use concise tables rather than prose recap.

### Timeline and location

Track date/time, elapsed duration, viewpoint, location, travel constraints, and
concurrent events. Flag impossible overlaps and unexplained travel.

### Knowledge and secrets

Track what each relevant character knows, believes, suspects, lies about, and
cannot know yet. Distinguish fact from a character's mistaken interpretation.

### Character state

Track injuries, clothing or visible changes when consequential, possessions,
commitments, resources, emotional residue, relationship state, and current goal.
Avoid inventorying details that cannot create a contradiction.

### Objects and world state

Track introduced objects, ownership/location, condition, rules/costs already
demonstrated, and irreversible environmental or institutional changes.

### Promises and payoffs

For each mystery, threat, desire, image, rule, prophecy, clue, or thematic
question, record plant location, reader expectation, planned payoff window,
actual payoff, and status: open, complicated, paid, or deliberately unresolved.

## Chapter handoff

Before drafting the next chapter provide the lead writer:

- accepted bible version;
- current outline and scene cards;
- previous accepted chapter or a compact necessary-context excerpt;
- continuity delta from the previous chapter;
- open promises entering this chapter;
- facts the viewpoint character knows and does not know;
- style controls and explicit exclusions.

After drafting, return:

- chapter Markdown;
- proposed bible changes;
- continuity ledger updates;
- new, advanced, or paid promises;
- outline discoveries and downstream impacts;
- uncertainties requiring user or lead-writer decision.

No worker may silently edit the bible or canonical chapter while another writer
continues from stale state.
