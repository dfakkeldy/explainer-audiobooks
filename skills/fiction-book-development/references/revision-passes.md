# Fiction Revision Passes

Revision moves from expensive, manuscript-wide decisions to local prose. Diagnose
one pass at a time, record findings, and let the lead writer make substantive
changes. Later polish must not disguise an unresolved earlier failure.

## Findings record

For each pass record:

- target chapter(s) or manuscript version;
- finding with exact location;
- consequence for reader or story;
- proposed repair class, not replacement prose unless requested;
- lead-writer decision: accept, reject, defer, or supersede;
- changed bible, outline, ledger, and downstream chapters.

## Pass 1: Premise and structure

Ask:

- Does the opening activate the promised story soon enough?
- Does opposition intensify because of choices, not arbitrary interruption?
- Do major turns close old options and create new pressure?
- Is the crisis the accumulated result of the story's decisions?
- Does the climax test the central character change or refusal?
- Does the ending deliver, complicate, or deliberately deny the genre promise?
- Can a chapter or subplot disappear without consequence?

Repair order: missing causality, misplaced turns, redundant threads, then scene
allocation. Stop if the premise itself cannot sustain the intended length.

## Pass 2: Character and causality

For each principal character verify desire, tactics, resistance, cost, and change
across the manuscript. Flag convenience: action taken only because the plot needs
it, withheld information without motive, sudden competence, unexplained
passivity, or transformation unsupported by prior pressure.

Strengthen choices rather than adding explanatory backstory. Ensure opposing
forces retain credible goals and can make intelligent moves.

## Pass 3: Scene function

For every scene record viewpoint, immediate goal, resistance, turn, changed
state, and downstream consequence. Cut, combine, or redesign scenes with no
change. Check entrances for delayed starts and exits for explanatory tails.
Balance dramatized moments with purposeful summary.

## Pass 4: Continuity and world logic

Audit the ledgers against manuscript text:

- timeline, travel, location, season, and concurrent events;
- who knows, suspects, believes, or cannot know each fact;
- injuries, objects, resources, commitments, and relationship states;
- world-rule costs, limits, exceptions, and enforcement;
- planted clues, promises, motifs, foreshadowing, and payoffs.

Accepted manuscript text is evidence; reconcile the bible after repairs. Do not
invent an off-page explanation merely to preserve a contradiction.

## Pass 5: Pacing and tension

Map pressure across chapters and within scenes. Look for repeated beat shapes,
back-to-back setup, premature aftermath, absent recovery, delayed consequences,
and climactic events with insufficient space. Tension can arise from uncertainty,
dread, desire, social risk, moral conflict, intimacy, wonder, or dramatic irony;
do not reduce pacing to action frequency.

Vary chapter and scene length because the story's pressure changes, not to create
random texture.

## Pass 6: POV and dialogue

Audit every perception and inference against the viewpoint contract. Remove
unmarked knowledge leakage and accidental distance shifts. If multiple POVs are
allowed, verify every switch follows the chosen convention and earns its access.

For dialogue, hide tags and test whether voices remain distinguishable. Check
immediate aims, unequal knowledge, tactics, subtext, interruptions, silence, and
status. Remove exchanges that only transfer information both speakers already
know.

## Pass 7: Prose and image system

Apply the project's style controls. Replace vague abstraction with precise action
or perception where it matters. Remove redundant emotional translation,
filter-word clutter, generic sensory lists, repeated sentence scaffolds, and
metaphors that compete or mix unintentionally. Preserve deliberate plainness,
repetition, fragmentation, dialect, and rhythm.

Track recurring images and charged objects: each recurrence should repeat,
intensify, invert, or complete meaning—not merely decorate another paragraph.
Do not run multiple global humanizers; they can flatten character voice and
create synonym cycling.

## Pass 8: Read-aloud and final canon

Read the full manuscript without editing first. Mark only points of confusion,
unintended drag, false emphasis, awkward sound, unclear speaker, broken promise,
or emotional effect that arrives before its cause. Repair the smallest sufficient
unit, then reconcile story bible and all ledgers to final canon.

## Completion gate

Revision is complete when:

- all passes have accepted/rejected records;
- no structural finding is hidden beneath line polish;
- every accepted repair's downstream impact is reconciled;
- character motives support decisive actions;
- continuity and world-rule spot checks pass;
- dialogue and POV retain distinct, controlled voices;
- promises are paid, complicated, or deliberately unresolved;
- final bible, ledgers, chapters, and combined Markdown agree.
