## 3. Turn a task into evidence

Use this formula:

**Action + what you handled + conditions or scale + useful result**

Weak: Helped customers.

Stronger: Handled customer questions and payments during busy meal periods, clarified orders, and resolved routine problems without delaying the line.

Weak: Did cleaning.

Stronger: Prepared and cleaned guest rooms to daily standards, restocked supplies, reported maintenance issues, and adjusted priorities for early arrivals.

Weak: Helped with appointments.

Stronger: Coordinated a changing family appointment and transportation schedule, maintained accurate notes, and communicated updates to several services.

## 17. My 30-day plan

### Days 1–3: Decide what better means

- [ ] Complete my job-quality floor.
- [ ] Define local, commutable, and remote zones.
- [ ] Choose two primary lanes and one reserve lane.

### Days 4–7: Build my base

- [ ] Complete eight evidence cards.
- [ ] Draft one base resume.
- [ ] Prepare five interview stories.
- [ ] Ask two references.
- [ ] Save my current source list.
- [ ] Solve one technology, printing, document, clothing, or transport barrier.

### Week 2: Begin a focused search

- [ ] Check saved sources twice.
- [ ] Verify and score real opportunities.
- [ ] Submit one to three strong, tailored applications as the market and my capacity allow.
- [ ] Make two precise information contacts.
- [ ] Practise one interview aloud.

### Week 3: Continue and test

- [ ] Repeat the strongest search and application actions.
- [ ] Follow up only when the stated timeline has passed.
- [ ] Test one new search term, source, reserve lane, or verified bridge.
- [ ] Keep searching while an application or interview is pending.

### Week 4: Review and adjust

- [ ] Count verified matches, applications, conversations, responses, interviews, and offers.
- [ ] Identify the main bottleneck.
- [ ] Revise one part of the lane, resume, evidence, search boundary, or bridge.
- [ ] Choose the next 30-day plan.

My low-energy action list:

1. Verify one saved posting.
2. Improve one evidence sentence.
3. Send one reference or information message.
4. Save one deadline or employer phone number.
5. Work for thirty focused minutes, record the next action, and stop.
