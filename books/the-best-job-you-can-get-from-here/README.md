# The Best Job You Can Get From Here

**Subtitle:** A Practical Job-Search Guide for Inverness County Without a Degree

**Author:** Dan Fakkeldy

**Status:** Public-safe version 0 for editorial and local review

**Edition research checked:** July 13, 2026

This folder contains an accelerated longform draft created with the repository’s explainer-audiobook workflow. It is substantial enough for revision but is not the final public edition.

## Review files

- `manuscript-v0.md` — 10 chapters plus introduction; approximately 20,000 spoken words and 2.2 hours at normal narration speed.
- `worksheets-v0.md` — printable practical tools, scripts, resume structures, tracker, interview preparation and 30-day plan.
- `source-notes-v0.md` — current-source ledger separating verified facts from editorial advice.
- `local-review-questions.md` — questions that need interviews or lived-experience review; no answers or anecdotes were invented.
- `development-notes.md` — brief, research and currency plan, visual direction and publication recommendation.

## Publication status

The book is classified as public-safe, but version 0 should not be described as final or publication-ready. Before release it needs:

1. the author’s additions and requested changes;
2. review by local job seekers and community or employment partners;
3. a fresh check of every perishable wage, listing, service, transit and program claim;
4. a bounded prose and accessibility edit;
5. three cover candidates and explicit author selection; and
6. final EPUB, source-note and distribution validation.

The coverless EPUB generated during manuscript assembly remains in the ignored build directory and is not a deliverable.
