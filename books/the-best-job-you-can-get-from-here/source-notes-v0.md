## Dated posting examples

These examples teach how to read postings. They are not endorsements, promises, representative wage claims, or a current vacancies list.

### NSCC Program Assistant, Wind Turbine Technician

Source: https://jobs.capebretonpartnership.com/jobs/18974

Snapshot on July 13, 2026:

- Port Hawkesbury; full-time; posted July 3; listed closing July 14, 2026.
- Displayed salary range: $51,258.30–$60,185.22.
- Required a relevant post-secondary industrial electrical or mechanical qualification, five years of recent industrial experience, ability to work at heights and outdoors, and other role-specific conditions.

Lesson: a title that sounds broadly administrative can contain firm technical, experience and physical requirements. Read the whole posting.

### YMCA After School Program Staff

Source: https://jobs.capebretonpartnership.com/jobs/18979

Snapshot on July 13, 2026:

- Part-time or casual, up to 17.5 hours per week; sites included Inverness and Port Hawkesbury; posted July 7; listed closing July 24, 2026.
- Displayed pay: $22 per hour.
- Required experience with children, first aid and CPR, and named screening checks. Post-secondary training was described as an asset rather than a requirement.

Lesson: distinguish required from preferred education, and compare weekly hours and screening costs or timing with the headline hourly rate.

### Remote transcription contractor

Source: https://jobs.capebretonpartnership.com/jobs/18982

Snapshot on July 13, 2026:

- Work from home; contractor; paid per page; posted July 9; listed closing July 23, 2026.
- Listed typing and software requirements, background screening, and exposure to potentially graphic material; said previous transcription experience was not required.

Lesson: “remote” does not necessarily mean employee status, hourly pay or easy work. Convert piece rates into a cautious real-hour estimate and consider emotional demands, equipment, privacy and tax status.

### Cheticamp Boatbuilders — Welder

Source: https://jobs.capebretonpartnership.com/jobs/18980

Snapshot on July 13, 2026:

- Full-time permanent role in Chéticamp.
- Listed Red Seal welder certification or comparable experience and described physical and confined-space demands.

Lesson: some postings explicitly leave room for comparable experience, but that does not erase the need for demonstrable technical competence and safe working ability.

## Remote-work scams

### Competition Bureau Canada — Job and employment scams

Source: https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/fraud-and-scams/tips-and-advice/job-and-employment-scams

Verified:

- Warning signs include guaranteed income, requests to pass payments through a personal account, fake-cheque or secret-shopper arrangements, and upfront fees for software, materials or training.
- Recommends researching the organization and not providing banking or credit-card information to an unknown party.

Editorial use: teach a pause-and-verify routine. Do not suggest every contractor or equipment requirement is fraudulent.
