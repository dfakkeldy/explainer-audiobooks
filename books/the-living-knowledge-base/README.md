# The Living Knowledge Base
_LLM Wikis, Research Notebooks, and Company Memory_

![Cover](cover.png)

> A good AI knowledge system is not just a pile of files with a chatbot on top. It is a maintained memory: raw sources preserved, durable synthesis written down, and clear rules telling the agent how to keep the whole thing honest.

This is a public-safe, narration-ready guide to Andrej Karpathy's LLM Wiki pattern, Open Notebook-style research workflows, embeddings, markdown knowledge bases, and the small-business use case for searchable company memory.

It explains the difference between RAG, a maintained wiki, and search; why plain markdown and git still matter; what "embedded" means in a research notebook; where Open Notebook fits; what security questions appear when private company data enters the system; and how a first pilot could help a local contractor or small operator without naming any private company or exposing client material.

## What you'll learn

How the three-layer LLM Wiki pattern works: raw sources, wiki, and schema. Why markdown plus frontmatter can be enough for serious memory. How Open Notebook complements, rather than replaces, a durable wiki. What embeddings do, and why embedding private material is a security decision. How a generic job binder could turn tenders, estimates, safety paperwork, supplier quotes, photos, and closeout records into reviewable operational memory. And why the first real pilot should be narrow, boring, and useful.

## Who it's for

Curious builders, consultants, small-business operators, and AI-agent users who want a practical mental model for turning scattered sources into maintained knowledge without handing the whole job to a black box.

This short book is grounded in public-source concepts and tools: Andrej Karpathy's LLM Wiki gist, Open Notebook documentation, Open Knowledge Format, NotebookLM-style retrieval, qmd-style markdown search, and general document-control patterns for small operating businesses. The contractor example is intentionally generic and public-safe.
