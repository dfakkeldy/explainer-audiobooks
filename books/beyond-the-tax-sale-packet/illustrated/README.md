# Beyond the Tax-Sale Packet — illustrated edition (2026)

A plain-language, illustrated edition of the 2026 book, for print and e-readers.

- `beyond-the-tax-sale-packet-illustrated.pdf` — US Letter, 149 pages, 13.3 MB
- `beyond-the-tax-sale-packet-illustrated.epub` — EPUB 3, 12.9 MB, EPUBCheck 5.4.0: 0 errors, 0 warnings

Status: **draft for Dan's review** (requested 2026-10-23, dfakkeldy/grok-bots#668). It is not
yet governed-final and has no narration. The first-edition package one folder up (EPUB, M4B,
alignment) is unchanged; its audiobook narrates the first-edition wording.

## What it is

- The same 13 chapters, facts and composite cases as the 2026 edition, rewritten in plain
  language. Sentences carrying a negative construction ("X is not Y", "this does not mean…"):
  555 of 2,317 in the first edition (23%), 50 of 1,838 here (3%).
- 39 figures: 31 new print infographics (process timelines, decision trees, a plan-view access
  drawing, charts of official sale results including 277 sales from 25 municipal events), plus two
  illustrations and six NS Marks The Spot screenshots kept from the first edition.
- A footer on every page that spells out that page's abbreviations and defines new words, and an
  official-source list at the end of each chapter.

Facts are as of July 2026, as in the first edition; the historical-results chart uses NS Marks The
Spot's catalogue at revision `43e40b0b8` (October 2026).

Source, rebuild instructions, the rewrite brief, per-chapter rewrite notes and the open legal
questions are in [`docs/nova-scotia-tax-sale-illustrated/`](../../../docs/nova-scotia-tax-sale-illustrated/).
